# Data sources

Status: work in progress, date 2026-11-04. Owned by `data-steward`.

**This is a factual summary of documents, not legal advice.** Eligibility under each venue's terms is for the owner to
confirm. Anything not opened is marked `UNVERIFIED`. A document that says nothing on a point is marked `SILENT`, and
silence is not read as permission.

Decision `D-17` makes possible commercial use the deciding scenario.

## Findings so far (being extended)

| Source | Finding from the document | Read |
|---|---|---|
| Binance Vision | Non-commercial only (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). Commercial use needs a separate written licence. Out under `D-17` | [Dataset Terms, v1.0, 2026-08-26](https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data/blob/master/TERMS_AND_CONDITIONS.md) |
| OKX | Market data use limited to personal, non-commercial trading and account management. No publishing or display to third parties, even free. Applies to public endpoints too | [API Agreement, 2026-07-28, section 9.4](https://www.okx.com/en-us/help/okx-api-agreement) |
| Kraken | General terms bar commercial exploitation and third-party availability of site content, scraping and automation. No separate market data licence was found | [Global Terms, 2026-10-30, section 9](https://www.kraken.com/legal/global-terms) |
| Gate | Licence limited to non-commercial or personal use. Resale or commercial purposes need written agreement | [User Agreement, 2026-07-22, clauses 10.1, 16.2](https://www.gate.com/legal/user-agreement) |
| Tardis | Paid-style licence with an express right to publish statistics and candles of 10 minutes or longer. Coinbase data excluded. Free access covers the first day of each month only | [Terms, modified 2026-10-28, clause 9.3, 23](https://docs.tardis.dev/legal/terms-of-service) |

All other sources: `UNVERIFIED` until this document is completed.
