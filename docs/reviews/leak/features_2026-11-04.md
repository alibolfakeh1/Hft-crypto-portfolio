# Leak audit: feature documents

Date: 2026-11-04. Auditor: `leak-auditor`, independent of the author.

Artifact: `docs/02_features/GATE_SPEC.md`, `STRATEGY_FEATURE_MATRIX.md`, `FEATURE_TRIAGE.md` and `DECISION_POINT_MAP.md`, as committed for this audit. Nothing has been coded, so this audits what a faithful implementation of the text would do.

The only question here is whether a value could have been known at decision time. Nothing here says whether a feature would make money.

## 1. Verdict

| Document | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Overall** | **`FAIL`** | Five high-severity findings. No gate, flow exit or `.L` backtest may be coded until `GATE_SPEC.md` and `STRATEGY_FEATURE_MATRIX.md` are fixed and audited again |
| `GATE_SPEC.md` | `FAIL` | A `.L` backtest sees bars and trades a run could not have (H1, H2). Evaluation design reads outcomes before freezing (H3, H4). It is also named in H5 and in nearly every medium and low finding |

Independently of these verdicts, `.L` results stay blocked by the documents' own rule until check D9 names a poll source, and `.R` flow research has no approved source under `D-17`.

Severity: **High** means a faithful implementation uses information not available at decision time, or reads test outcomes before a selection. **Medium** means the text lets a developer implement a leak, or the comparison is biased or breaks a hard rule. **Low** means a small effect or a contradiction to clear up.

## 2. Rulings the author asked for

| Question | Ruling |
|---|---|
| `pct` and `E_W` recomputed at each run are causal indicators under item 10 (GATE_SPEC 4, 15) | **Upheld, on conditions.** Both are fixed-window statistics recomputed from earlier data, and they passed truncation and future perturbation on synthetic data. Conditions: M9 (each reference member uses its own point-in-time normaliser), L1 (the reference excludes the ranked window), M8 (the state a run needs is declared correctly) |
| "The question of whether a week is a valid block does not arise" | **Holds only for `pct` and `E_W`.** The block question remains for every fitted object: `v_min`, the `F39-X2` rank map and limits, the `JSmax_60` limit, the `F41-X1` minimum scale, and any grid result. Their block is the calendar quarter of the fold plan, and each must be frozen at the quarter's start. A rolling `F19-X1` fit is also a causal indicator, but only under M11. Declared constants (`tau_b`, the ladder, the 0.5 and 0.25 rank cuts on `RR`) count as frozen, because they were fixed before any data. `tau_p` has no declared first-pass value (M10) |
| The availability rule in GATE_SPEC 3.1, with a 10 s allowance | **Not accepted as written.** An exchange-clock window plus an availability check at the run is the right structure. The allowance is anchored on the wrong time, applied only in backtests and unmeasured. See H1 |
| Flow acting at runs between 4h closes (GATE_SPEC 6.1) | **Causal, and allowed.** Inputs end at `t_last`, the action starts at `t_dec + latency`, and `C_b` and `ATR_b` are older closed values. C17's "only resolve touches" should read: the candle model computes nothing at those runs, and only the flow overlay may act at step 4. Steps 2 and 3 stay ahead of it, so no ordering problem arises beyond M4, M6 and M7. No data is double-counted in the leak sense. Under `C-LATE`, run spacing is 17 to 103 minutes, so consecutive 60-minute windows can share up to 43 minutes of flow. That is a multiple-look cost to report, not future information |
| Does `WAIT` use later data correctly? | **Yes, as written.** Each look recomputes on its own `t_last` and no outcome enters. The trap is M1: the published look table can only be reproduced by reading the realised next run time, which a run cannot know |
| Does the trigger bar's flow double-count the setup? | **Not a future leak.** It is known at the run and the document declares it. It is a confound, handled by R4 and the C15 placebo. See L5 for the one gap |
| Flow may never widen a stop, extend a time stop or extend a target | **The text complies, but two paths break it.** M4 can leave the stop under `VX` wider than under `V0`. M5 raises a fill-based target through `WAIT`. Time stops are clean |
| Is `flow_venue` chosen before results? | **For `.L`, yes in principle, with gaps. For `.R`, no longer**, since `D-17` removed the registered choice and nothing replaced it. See M14 |

## 5. Checked and found clean

- **Window form.** `(t_last - W, t_last]` on the exchange clock, on `(open, close]` bars, is causal and ends before `t_dec`. `F18-X1`'s start price comes from the minute ending at `t - W`, outside the window.
- **Synthetic truncation and future perturbation.** `B_60`, `E_60`, `NS_60`, `pct`, `PRE_60`, `F19-X1` with a fit ending at `t0`, `JSmax_60` and `F23-X1` were all identical after truncation and after randomising later data. A centred-window control failed, as it should.
- **Adaptations against the canonical cards.** For F18, F19, F25, F26, F27, F28, F41 and F42, the stated changes do not alter causality as claimed, apart from M11. The `F41-X1` scale ends before the current return. `F25-X1` adds the current burst only after scoring. `F26-X1` closes on a later timestamp, with no retroactive grouping. `F28-X1` is a to-date stock at a landmark, with elasticity kept as a label.
- **Run order.** Stops and targets resolve before flow, and a flow decision cannot affect bars it read. No back-dating, no replay of missed looks, and `A_max` and `expiry` run from `t_k`. Rule order in 5.3 and the idempotency key are sound.
- **Exit rules.** `TIGHTEN`'s ratchet and clamp cannot lower a stop against the stop it starts from (M4 is about which stop that is). `R` is fixed. Flow has no part in time stops. I searched the strategy documents for targets or time stops keyed to the stop's level or to a scale-out, and found only S08's `T2` decay keyed to the `T1` fill, which a flow scale-out is not.
- **Conflation.** Conflated buckets are never treated as events. `D-OF31-X1` is net per bucket, and features needing events stay `LATER` or `DROP`.
- **Next-day archive in `.R`.** Treating `T2` as available at `exchange_ts + A` in simulation is acceptable as labelled, because `.R` is barred from the live record, subject to M12. `.L` excludes `T2` as a decision input, subject to M22.
- **Kinds and first pass.** The label rule is sound: an archive whose source has not passed D9 is `.R`. First-pass thresholds are declared without outcomes. Fitted items in GATE_SPEC 8 use no outcomes. `F35-X1` is evaluation only. Reversed results are not adopted, and rejected rows are not replaced without new data.
- **Arithmetic.** The second and third look percentages, the sum to 64, the power table and the worked example all recompute as stated.
