# Leak audit: candle strategy documents

Date: 2026-11-04. Auditor: `leak-auditor`. Artifact: `docs/01_strategies/`, `00_INDEX.md` and `S01` to `S10`, read in
full as they stood on disk (543, 320, 327, 348, 364, 319, 288, 310, 330, 282 and 283 lines). Charter rulings were read
in `docs/00_CHARTER.md`. Nothing has been coded, so every finding is about what a faithful implementation of the text
would do. All prices in this review are invented or synthetic.

## 1. Verdict

**Overall: `FAIL`.** The ten strategy rules are causal in their own definitions, and that part of the set held up well.
The failure is in the shared conventions in `00_INDEX.md`, which every document inherits. A faithful implementation
would cancel entries using the price they would have filled at, and would select parameters on trades that run into the
test block. Swing lists with replacement have a join that passes the prescribed timestamp audit while still using later
data.

All fixes are text edits. No strategy needs redesign. Only changed text needs a new audit.

- `HIGH`: a faithful implementation, or the plain reading of an instruction, makes a decision or fill depend on data not

  reproducible, or leaves a wrong audit trail.

## 3. Charter rulings

No ruling creates a future leak by itself. Two of them expose problems in the index text, and one creates an accounting
distortion.

| Ruling | Finding |
|---|---|
| **Item 10, rolling indicators against fitted objects** | **Accepted, along with C12's reading of rolling indicators.** `VR`, `SRV`, `BBW` and `PR` use windows ending at or before the bar and have no fitted state. `VR` and `PR` include the current bar in their own normaliser. That is causal and needs no freeze, and `SRV` excludes it. Truncation tests found no mismatches where run. Two notes: Wilder `ATR` is recursive and not fixed-window, which is harmless for causality (L-34), and C12's "only one fitted object" conflicts with the ruling's last sentence (L-23). |
| **Item 13, resting paper stops** | **Accepted, with no future leak of its own.** The model uses only levels set before the touch. It does have consequences: targets become passive fills, so the C6 waiver of G5 is false (L-18), and an order cannot rest before `t_dec + latency`, so the straddling minute must be fixed (L-05). The stop and target are also assumed to rest from a fill that the run may not see until the next run, and to resize after a scale-out with no failure. That is fairness and not leak, and the item already requires reports to say so. Every `CYCLE` chart and table should carry that label. G8 cannot test it, as no orders exist. |
| **Section 7 rule 4, size against `R`** | **Accepted for decision timing.** Size uses the decision price in C6 and C17, and no document uses a fill or a later open for size. `R` is used only after the fill. However, `R` from the fill has no floor, so results in `R` can be inflated by a post-decision price, and "comparable in `R` multiples" fails for those trades (L-20). A fill at or beyond `S0` under the L-01 fix has no valid `R`, and L-20 covers that as well. |
| **Section 7 rule 7, time stops on the cycle** | **Accepted as written.** Taking effect at the first run and filling after it is causal, and step 5 and the time fields implement it. Read with C17's order of work, a dropped run can cancel a due time stop using later prices, which is what the rule says must not happen (L-06). I also suggest reporting the result earned during overruns, as it can be positive. |
| **`V0S` maximum hold** | **Accepted, with no leak.** `m` and `H` are stated in each document and set at entry, S09 states its use of the hard flat, and the target from the fill is stated in C13. Conditions: `m` and `H` are frozen constants and any change is a counted trial, as they are outside the grids and they decide what "dynamic management adds". Under the cycle, `H` follows rule 7. `V0S` inherits L-09 and L-17. |

Index open questions 1, 2, 3 and 5 are now answered by these rulings and should be closed in the text.

## 5. Notes, not findings

- **Venue, under `D-17` and `D-01`.** The research source and venue are undecided. Binance wording in C10, C16, the
  index sources and S03 section 13, and the 00:00 funding reason for S09's `T_flat`, are venue-specific notes. S09's
  `T_flat` is `h_end + 11`, so it only sits before funding on some schedules. Bar labels must be mapped by `data-steward`.
  Treating a bar's open-time label as its knowable time would be a one-bar leak, so test this at G1 on the chosen source.
- **`D-12` against C17.** Canonical bars come from trades, while `CYCLE` builds from 1m bars. A difference between them
  would enter `IDEAL` minus `CYCLE`. Reconcile at G1.
- **Text with no leak.** S04 section 14 says a delay never shifts a test to a different bar, but a late fill can make
  `n = 1` a later bar than in `IDEAL`. S02 section 14 says the roll rule "acts as usual" for a position not yet open at
  the roll. S09's labels for hour `h`, `[h_s, h_end)` and `h_peak` are coherent only if hours are read by open time, and
  no reading I tried leaks, but it should be stated. C5's `0.5 * ATR_k` expiry and C17's per-strategy chase differ for
  flow variants, which is for the `docs/02_features/` audit.
- **Regime tables.** Day types "measured afterwards" must stay report labels. S01 says this outright. A gate using them
