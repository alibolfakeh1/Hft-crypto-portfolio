## 2. Coverage and a failing quarter

Declared before any coverage is read. `data-steward` applies this once, from counts alone, before the first outcome, and
the result is appended to the decision log.

**Coverage rule, per quarter, on both symbols:** unproved empty minutes under 1%, and at least 100 trades in each of 90% of
hours, the figures in `GATE_SPEC.md` 8.2. Only pass or fail and the empty-minute counts leave the check. A later numeric R6
for flow does not move these dates.

| Case | What happens |
|---|---|
| A required series starts after 2020-01-01, or the first quarter fails | `T_start` moves to the start of the first quarter where all series exist and the quarter passes. Only the head of the history can move it |
| A later quarter in the first span fails | No date moves. Its bars stay flagged and its setups are not used |
| A test quarter fails | It is not a test block and is not read. No date moves. Later training leaves it out |
| Fewer than 4 test quarters pass | The last passing quarters of the first span become test quarters, latest first, until there are 4, while the first span keeps at least 6 passing quarters. This is possible only before the first outcome. Afterwards, quarters from 2026-10-01 join as new dated blocks instead |
| A locked quarter fails | It is left out and named. The block does not move and does not borrow from anything |
| Fewer than 2 locked quarters pass, the first span has under 6 passing quarters, or no test block can be formed | `BLOCKED`. It goes to the owner, and no run starts |

So there is always at least one test block, or the plan says `BLOCKED` before any outcome exists.

## 3. One rule for reading

**Read** means any price, return, flow or outcome from a date reaching a person, an agent, a report, a chart, a log, or a
calculation that can change a decision.

**Not a read:** listings, downloads, checksums and sizes. Counts of files, rows, ids and empty minutes with and without
proof. Pass or fail by quarter, and row checks that output only a flag. A check may use values inside itself, as long as
only these leave it. They may run on any date.

| Dates | Rule |
|---|---|
| First training span | Open. Each reading is logged and counted, under `GATE_SPEC.md` 8.3 |
| Test blocks | Closed until the owner opens them in writing. Opened in calendar order, with no skipping and candidates frozen first. Each verdict is recorded with its date. A row, grid, sensitivity or constant chosen after that date is `EXPLORATORY` on every block read by the verdict that led to it, and can earn `SUPPORTED` only on later blocks or at the locked read |
| Locked block | Read **once, globally,** at the final verdict, on the owner's written decision, for every candidate frozen that day, all counted. A candidate frozen later gets no read. Nothing reads it before then: not `V0`, G3, G7, a portfolio report, a benchmark, an app or a demonstration |
| Later quarters | The locked block never extends. They join only as new dated blocks, under the test block rule. Until the locked block is read, its dates enter no training |

**What holds this in place.**

- The reader refuses any date after the last opened date, today 2023-12-31. `developer` builds this, with a test. Every
  report, including the benchmark (DR-08), stops at the same date.
- A setup whose `t_k + H_max` passes the last opened date is left out of that read and counted, as in C18, so no trade reads
  an unopened date and removal does not depend on the outcome. It comes back when the next block opens.
- A read by mistake is logged the same day. Its days join section 4 and are treated the same way.

**What this means, plainly.**

- **Neither route in N6 was taken at the time.** Pilot runs were not confined to dates before the first test block. The other
  route, logging every day and computing no strategy outcome on them, can hold only from here on, because the demonstration did
  compute a rule's outcomes. It also ran before C-3 and C-5, and the strategy re-audit defines the first run as "the first
  simulation of any strategy, control or placebo on any market data from any source". `D-27` records it as not a trial because
  its rule is not one of S01 to S10. `leak-auditor` should say whether that stands.
- **The days are excluded.** Their bars stay in the data, because indicators need continuity. No S01 to S10 outcome is

  trial. If anything from the demonstration's rule enters a candidate, that is a counted trial and these days count as used.

## 6. Rulings

- **Expanding training: yes.** An earlier test block joins the training of later blocks only, once read, under the C18 purge.
  It selects nothing for itself or for an earlier block. A fixed window would add a free length to choose. Charter item 11 needs
  this sentence, which is outside this change: "A finished test block may join the training of later blocks only, under the same
  purge."
- **Placebo calendar (N1).** Within the first span, quarters act as blocks, and each draws only from earlier ones. 2020-Q1 has no
  level placebo for S01, S02, S04, S05, S06 or S08, and reports say so. 2024-Q1 may draw on the whole first span.
- **Trial count (H5, the part for `project-manager`).** A line pooled over two symbols counts twice, as the matrix says. The index's
  "each flow variant multiplies" is replaced by the matrix's lines on one frozen configuration. A second mode on the same line is a
  report, as long as no selection reads both. Pooled `N` stays with `feature-integrator`.
- **Adoption (N4).** The roadmap adopts section 3 now. Still to adopt it: C15 and C18 (`strategy-architect`), and `GATE_SPEC.md` 8.2,
  8.3 and 8.6 and matrix 3.1, where "for this question" becomes "by the verdict that led to this row" (`feature-integrator`).
