## 2. Coverage and a failing quarter

Declared before any coverage was read. `data-steward` applies this **once per bar source**, from counts alone, before that
source's first outcome, and the result is appended to the decision log.

**Coverage rule, per quarter, on both symbols:** unproved empty minutes under 1%, and at least 100 trades in each of 90% of
hours, the figures in `GATE_SPEC.md` 8.2. Only pass or fail and the empty-minute counts leave the check. A later numeric R6

| Case | What happens |

So there is always at least one test block, or the plan says `BLOCKED` before any outcome exists.

## 3. One rule for reading

**Read** means any price, return, flow, funding rate or outcome from a date, or anything computed from these, reaching a person, an
agent, a report, a chart, a log, or a calculation that can change a decision.

**Not a read:** listings, downloads, checksums and sizes. Counts of files, rows, ids and empty minutes with and without
proof. Pass or fail by quarter, and row checks that output only a flag. A check may use values inside itself, as long as
only these leave it. They may run on any date. **Counts, sizes and flags on unopened dates may decide only coverage and storage. A
rule, constant or sample chosen from them is a read.**

| Dates | Rule |
|---|---|
| First training span | Open. Each reading is logged and counted, under `GATE_SPEC.md` 8.3 |
| Test blocks | Closed until the owner opens them in writing. Opened in calendar order, with no skipping and candidates frozen first. Each verdict is recorded with its date. A row, grid, sensitivity or constant chosen after that date is `EXPLORATORY` on every block read by the verdict that led to it, and can earn `SUPPORTED` only on later blocks or at the locked read. **A quarter read on candles is `EXPLORATORY` for all ten strategies and every flow line, not only the eight that ran** |
| Locked block | Read **once, globally,** at the final verdict, on the owner's written decision, for every candidate frozen that day, all counted. A candidate frozen later gets no read. Nothing reads it before then: not `V0`, G3, G7, a portfolio report, a benchmark, an app or a demonstration |
| Later quarters | The locked block never extends. They join only as new dated blocks, under the test block rule. Until the locked block is read, its dates enter no training |

**What holds this in place.**

- **The last opened date is 2023-12-31 (`D-34`),** and it stays there through all of stage 1. Opening a quarter on candles takes the
  written route in section 5, and the request says it spends that quarter for stage 2.
- **Unopened dates sit in their own root,** which `MarketReader` and `report` cannot open, and no bars are built there. The scheduled
  report stays off until this is in (FP-01, recheck section 10 item 0). One scheduled run could otherwise read and publish the
  whole locked block, and that cannot be undone. `developer` and `data-steward` build it.
- **Merging this branch to `main` arms the schedule,** because `main` holds no workflow today. So no merge until item 0 is in.
- **A date guard sits in the reader, the report, the benchmark and the portfolio view** (item 3a). Bars by close time, trades by stamp,
  funding by settlement time. Its value comes from the dated log entry, and a test compares the two. `developer` builds it.
- **A setup is left out when `t_k + H_max` reaches the first unopened instant,** using C18's word (FP-07). It is counted, no trade reads an
  unopened date, and removal does not depend on the outcome. So no funding stamped at 2024-01-01 00:00 is needed. It comes back when the
  next block opens.
- **A setup left out is never simulated.** The strategy counts as flat across it, so no later setup's `SKIPPED_OPEN` rests on it. It is
  logged with its reason. Dropping rows after simulating does not meet this (FP-06).
- **A read by mistake is logged the same day.** Its days join section 4 and are treated the same way.
